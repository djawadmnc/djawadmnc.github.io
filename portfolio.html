About me

From zero
to brand hero

I started with nothing but a laptop and an obsession with making things look right. No formal school, no connections — just thousands of hours learning, failing, and refining my craft one project at a time.

Today I work with startups, entrepreneurs, and established businesses to build visual identities that mean something. I don't just design logos — I build the language a brand speaks in, how it feels, and why people remember it.

Design is not decoration. It's the difference between being noticed and being forgotten. I'm here to make sure you're never forgotten.

— Djaouad
